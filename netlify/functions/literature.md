### paley-tetsworth-1992-uniapical

- **Authors:** Paley D, Tetsworth K
- **Title:** Mechanical axis deviation of the lower limbs. Preoperative planning of uniapical angular deformities of the tibia or femur.
- **Journal:** Clinical Orthopaedics and Related Research
- **Year:** 1992
- **PMID:** 1611764
- **DOI:** unknown
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1611764/
- **Anatomy:** generic (femur and tibia)
- **Deformity type:** uniapical coronal angular
- **Method:** Geometric preoperative planning on standing full-length radiographs
- **Key finding (1–2 sentences):** Established mechanical-axis-based preoperative planning of uniapical angular deformities. If the osteotomy is at the apex, only angulation is needed; if proximal or distal to the apex, secondary translation is also required.
- **Notes:** Foundational method for analysis of single-apex tibial/femoral angular deformities (human).

### paley-tetsworth-1992-multiapical

- **Authors:** Paley D, Tetsworth K
- **Title:** Mechanical axis deviation of the lower limbs. Preoperative planning of multiapical frontal plane angular and bowing deformities of the femur and tibia.
- **Journal:** Clinical Orthopaedics and Related Research
- **Year:** 1992
- **PMID:** 1611765
- **DOI:** unknown
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1611765/
- **Anatomy:** generic (femur and tibia)
- **Deformity type:** multiapical coronal / bowing
- **Method:** Geometric planning on standing radiographs
- **Key finding (1–2 sentences):** Defined planning rules for multiapical and bowing deformities (compensated vs non-compensated). Each apex must be identified using joint reference lines and mechanical axes.
- **Notes:** Companion paper to PMID 1611764 — covers complex bowing such as rickets, anterolateral and posteromedial tibial bow.

### paley-1994-cora

- **Authors:** Paley D, Herzenberg JE, Tetsworth K, McKie J, Bhave A
- **Title:** Deformity planning for frontal and sagittal plane corrective osteotomies.
- **Journal:** Orthopedic Clinics of North America
- **Year:** 1994
- **PMID:** 8028886
- **DOI:** unknown
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8028886/
- **Anatomy:** generic (femur and tibia)
- **Deformity type:** uniplanar coronal / uniplanar sagittal
- **Method:** Geometric planning of frontal- and sagittal-plane osteotomies (CORA framework)
- **Key finding (1–2 sentences):** Universal geometric system: the intersection of proximal and distal mechanical/anatomic axes defines the Center of Rotation of Angulation (CORA). Performing the osteotomy at the CORA prevents secondary translation.
- **Notes:** Defining paper for CORA methodology; foundation of modern deformity correction.
